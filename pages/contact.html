Frequently Asked Questions

What are the dimensions of King and Queen sets?

Our King duvet cover measures 240 × 260 cm with 50 × 75 cm pillowcases. Queen duvets measure 220 × 240 cm. All fitted sheets feature a deep 35 cm elasticized pocket that comfortably accommodates high-profile pillow-top mattresses.

How should I wash and care for hand-embroidered bedding?

We recommend washing on a delicate 30°C cycle with a mild liquid detergent. Wash inside-out to protect the embroidery threads. Line drying in natural shade preserves fabric softness and prevents shrinking.

What are the delivery timelines across India?

Major urban centers (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad/Rawalpindi) typically receive delivery within 3 to 4 business days. Regional and nationwide destinations arrive within 5 business days via insured express courier.

Can I customize or request bespoke monogramming?

Yes! For bridal registries or custom heirloom monograms, please reach out to our concierge via WhatsApp or through the inquiry form above with your desired thread color and initials.
